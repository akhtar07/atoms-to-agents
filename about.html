Research summary

I use DFT electronic-structure and bonding descriptors to predict macroscopic behaviour (slip-system selection and interstitial strengthening) in titanium and the HCP metals, and carry them up through molecular dynamics and crystal-plasticity simulation to experiment. I fine-tune machine-learned interatomic potentials (MACE, with GPU committee active learning on a 1,600+ VASP database), and I build multi-agent systems (the ICME Agent, ICME-lite and TiSci) that run, validate and interpret VASP, LAMMPS and DAMASK workflows with deterministic, regression-tested physics. I also write condensed-matter and semiconductor-transport problems for frontier-AI scientific benchmarks (SciCode v2, Agentic SciCode).

Research projects & software

2025–26

ICME Agent

~55-agent, ~68k-line platform running VASP, LAMMPS and DAMASK on HPC; deterministic core, local Qwen2.5-32B narration, physics library (e.g. W thermal expansion 4.18 vs 4.5 ppm/K), closed-loop discovery, FastAPI + WebSocket GUI, 26 regression suites.
2026

TiSci

An auditable literature-to-calculation agent for titanium alloys: 203,063 works, 1.35M-node graph, 283 cycles over 228 h, verdicts in code, carried through ICME-lite to crystal plasticity.
2025–26

MACE for high-entropy alloys

Fine-tuning on 1,600+ VASP calculations with 10-member GPU committees; energy error 40.8 → 2.4 meV/atom, force 84.9 → 38.5 meV/Å.
