Generation 3 · flagship

TiSci: an auditable agent that chooses multiscale calculations from the literature

TiSci reads the titanium literature, builds a knowledge graph, proposes what a solute should do to α-Ti, picks the calculations worth running, runs them, and records why each idea was accepted or rejected. The point is auditability rather than discovery: you can trace every verdict back to a paper, a calculation and a rule.

Where the LLM loses to a simple rule. On 340 sign predictions, the LLM was right 262 times (77%); a no-LLM periodic-trend rule was right 282 times (83%, p = 0.019).
Where the surrogate is weak. Orb-v3 agrees with MACE in only 10 of 31 cells, and PBE reverses the CRSS sign for all four of Al, Nb, Si and Sn. MACE gets C44 of pure Ti at only 44% of experiment.
Choosing what to run. Expected-information-gain selection did no better than random. None of the 565 proposals was rated SOUND.

Reporting these is the point: an agent that is wrong in recorded, inspectable ways is more useful to a scientist than one that is confidently right with no trail.

Generation 2

ICME-lite: one sentence to crystal plasticity

“Compute the CRSS of hcp titanium and run a tension test.” ICME-lite turns that into DFT or MD, a semi-discrete Peierls–Nabarro model, and DAMASK + VPSC, for fcc Al, bcc W and hcp Ti, with no material-specific code.

  • The local LLM (qwen2.5:32b) only classifies a request into one of seven workflows; anything outside them is refused.
  • 41 critic rules over 16 result types check physics at every hand-off.
  • All 9 generalized-stacking-fault lines and all 10 screw-dislocation cores converged; W Peierls barrier 216 meV.
  • Fixing the elastic workflow cut the Ti C66 error from up to 91.9% to ≤ 4.9%.
Absolute CRSS is not reliable. Predicted / measured CRSS spans 0.73–557×. The core-width criterion ζ/b > 1 identifies the defensible cases, the same lesson as the manual study in chapter 4.

Generation 1

ICME Agent: a multi-agent platform for VASP, LAMMPS and DAMASK on HPC

A MasterSupervisor routes work to VASP, LAMMPS and DAMASK supervisors over a message bus. A deterministic core does the physics; a local LLM (Qwen2.5-32B, regex fallback) parses requests and narrates results. It grew to about 55 agents and 68k lines, with 26 regression suites.

Side branch · draft

SciAgent: a closed hypothesis–ICME loop, and how it fails silently

KGGen + Qwen3-8B turned a quarter-million documents into a graph; six stages and about thirty calculation types closed the loop from hypothesis to a checked calculation. Then I measured what the loop actually tests.

  • It rarely tests what it claims. 0 of 148 proposals were tested directly; the final cycle produced 0 of 64 SOUND.
  • Placement matters more than content. Measured facts were quoted in 2 of 60 or 22 of 60 proposals, depending only on where they sat in the prompt.
  • Numbers drift. LLM-produced quantities had a 34.3% median error against 8.6% for the reference path.
Why it matters. These silent failures are why TiSci keeps the verdict in code and every claim tied to a source. SciAgent is the negative result that shaped the design.
